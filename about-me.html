Char Kuey Tiaw Goreng

Ultimate comfort food!

Main Ingredients (Serves 2):
  • 200g Kuey Tiaw (Flat Rice Noodles)
  • 6-8 Fresh Prawns
  • 1 Bowl Bean Sprouts & Chives
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Tablespoon Chili Paste (adjust to taste)
Sauce (Mix well):
  • 2 Tablespoons Sweet Soy Sauce
  • 1 Tablespoon Dark Soy Sauce
  • 1 Teaspoon Oyster Sauce
Cooking Instructions:
  1. Heat oil in a wok over high heat. Sauté garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add prawns, stir until half cooked. Push to the side of the wok.
  3. Add kuey tiaw and the mixed sauce. Stir quickly.
  4. Push kuey tiaw to the side, crack the egg and fry briefly.
  5. Add bean sprouts and chives. Fry briefly, remove and serve hot!
